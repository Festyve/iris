# Web research 2026

Curated on 2026-11-22 to fill gaps in the original vault. Sources were selected for concrete interface or visual-system evidence, captured at a 1440 × 1000 desktop viewport, and stored under `assets/research/`. Screenshots are for internal design reference only; ownership remains with the linked creators and organizations.

## Selection criteria

- First-party product, design-system or documentation source where possible
- Clear visual evidence of interface, layout, components, type, colour or interaction
- Distinct contribution to the vault rather than a duplicate of an existing aesthetic
- Usable resolution and no authentication requirement
- Anonymous profile synthesis: no profile is named after an individual designer

## Limitations and use

- Screenshots represent the source at one date, viewport and loading state; revisit the original URL for current behavior and motion.
- Some pages use animation or interactive canvases that a still image cannot fully communicate.
- Third-party trademarks and copyrighted visuals remain the property of their owners. These captures are not product assets and should not be shipped or copied literally.
- Pinterest was available as a lead source, but the expansion deliberately favors first-party pages to improve attribution and reduce duplicates.
