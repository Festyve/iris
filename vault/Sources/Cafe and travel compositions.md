# Café and travel compositions

## Daybreak

The split headline and offset artwork use the broad margins and asymmetric grid from [[Swiss Grid]]. The counter cards combine compact prices with ingredients and a pickup action. The drink controls draw on the segmented selections in the Laras Espresso reference. The visit page borrows the neighbourhood guide's place-first organization. [Coffee controls and city-guide references](../Aesthetics/Mobile%20App%20Patterns.md)

All three café illustrations are original to this vault. Each page keeps navigation, prices, item names, selections, and opening hours as editable text or shapes. [Café pages](../Originals/pages/daybreak/home.svg)

## Paper Miles

Wanderlog inspired keeping a numbered itinerary and a matching map in view together. Rome2Rio informed the side-by-side comparison of transport choices. The map, route times, and saved lists are illustrative planning data. [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/) · [Rome2Rio journey search](https://help.rome2rio.com/en/support/solutions/articles/22000280935-how-to-search-for-a-journey-on-rome2rio)

Briefs can select Tokyo, Kyoto, Paris, London, Lisbon, or Copenhagen. The destination carries through the itinerary, route comparison and saved places. [Sources and page previews](../Originals/README.md)
