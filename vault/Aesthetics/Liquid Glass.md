# Liquid Glass

Mostly Apple-style "liquid glass" and visionOS work. Translucent rounded panels with heavy blur sit over photos of rooms, landscapes or blurred colour fields. There are also close-ups of single glass components (pill buttons, toggles, an upload button), a few frosted dashboards and phone screens, and light, pale grey-blue component kits.

## Type

Clean SF-Pro-like sans in light/regular weights. Very large thin numerals (9:41, 28°C, 21°) sit beside tiny labels, so scale contrast is high. No serifs.

## Signature moves

- Blurred translucent panels with a bright 1px inner rim and a soft drop shadow, floating over photography
- Pill-shaped capsule buttons and toggles with specular highlights (liquid glass)
- visionOS layout: a floating window, a separate bottom tab capsule and a left icon rail
- Widgets with oversized light numerals over a saturated blurred background
- Glass cards tinted by the colour behind them (the backdrop bleeds through)
- Curved or carousel galleries of panels in 3D space
