# Editorial and Luxury

Fashion, hospitality and studio websites in magazine style. Large serif or condensed headlines, warm neutral palettes, generous whitespace and full-bleed photography with asymmetric image grids. Some items are serif typeface promos.

## Type

High-contrast display serifs (Didone and transitional, often italic) at very large sizes, paired with small grotesk or mono captions. Some condensed grotesk caps (GAZU, STILL IN MOTION).

## Signature moves

- Oversized serif headline with italic word swaps
- Full-bleed editorial photography with small caption blocks
- Warm cream backgrounds and muted earthy accents
- Asymmetric magazine grids, images cropped at edges
- Thin rules and numbered sections, mono metadata
