# Mobile App Patterns

Contemporary iOS app concepts, mostly 2-3 phone mockups per shot. Includes dark apps with neon accents, minimal monochrome screens with dot-matrix numerals, illustrated onboarding, fintech, travel and AI assistants. Strong use of big typography, colour-blocked cards and bottom tab bars.

## Type

Grotesk sans in mixed weights with huge display numerals (11, 12:31, $3,840.75). Dot-matrix numerals on minimal screens. Condensed display caps (LOCAL FINDS) and occasional italic serif accents.

## Signature moves

- Dot-matrix numerals and calendars on minimal monochrome screens
- Colour-blocked cards (pink, yellow, green, blue) on black
- Giant geometric shapes (red circle) as data viz
- Illustrated onboarding cards with soft paper tones
- Floating pill tab bar and large greeting headline
