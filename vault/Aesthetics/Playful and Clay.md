# Playful and Clay

Friendly consumer apps: chunky rounded cards, candy pastel or neon fills, mascots and blob characters, and 3D clay icons. Includes mood trackers, habit lists, travel apps and onboarding screens. Several are pure 3D icon sets and claymorphism showcases.

## Type

Rounded or chunky grotesk sans, often heavy and condensed for headlines (WHAT'S YOUR MOOD LIKE TODAY). Large timer numerals, playful mixed-case. Some hand-drawn doodle lettering.

## Signature moves

- Mascot or blob characters with simple eyes as hero
- Thick rounded pill items in bright alternating colours
- 3D clay icons with soft pastel shading
- Sticker or collage overlays on app screens
- Strong two-colour phones (lime/black, orange/green)
