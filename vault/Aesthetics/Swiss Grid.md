# Swiss Grid

International Typographic Style posters and websites. They use strict grids, huge flush-left grotesk headlines, asymmetric layouts and lots of white. Accents are flat red, blue or green, sometimes with a dithered or gridded image texture. Websites show a modular grid, mono metadata and mountain or architecture photography.

## Type

Neo-grotesk sans (Helvetica/Neue Haas-like), mostly bold or medium at huge sizes with tight leading. Small mono or sans captions and numbered metadata give extreme scale contrast. Occasional stacked broken words (PAS / SES).

## Signature moves

- Visible modular grid lines used as a decorative layer
- Huge flush-left headlines with hard word breaks, small text blocks set off to the side
- Single flat accent field (red or blue) against black and white
- Dithered or gridded photo textures under type
- Numbered sections, dates and coordinates set as mono metadata
