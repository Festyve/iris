# Tactile and Skeuomorphic

Neumorphic and skeuomorphic interfaces: soft extruded light-grey controls, knobs, toggles and clocks with inner and outer shadows. A second strand is hardware-inspired design (Teenage Engineering TX-6/OP-1, TING), with dense icon glyph grids and colourful line UIs on black. There are also 3D renders of physical buttons, hands turning knobs, and pastel clay-like widgets.

## Type

Small neutral sans (Avenir/Inter-like) on neumorphic UI. Tight technical sans and mono with tiny all-caps labels on hardware pieces. Big thin numerals for time and temperature.

## Signature moves

- Dual light and dark soft shadows to extrude or deboss controls from the same-colour surface
- Physical metaphors: rotary knobs, slide faders, rocker switches, LEDs
- Teenage Engineering-style labelled glyph grids and instrument panels
- One saturated accent (orange or red) on monochrome grey hardware
- Clay or matte 3D renders with soft studio light
