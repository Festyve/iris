# Minimal Tech

Developer and AI-SaaS minimalism. There are dark navy or black landing pages with blue glows, light cream pages with isometric or line illustrations, and many mono-type product screens (thermostat, radio, clock). Phone UIs use a single strong accent (lime, orange, red) on neutral grounds. A large share of the set is monospace font specimens.

## Type

Monospace throughout (labels, specs and some headlines), paired with neutral grotesk sans for headlines. Editorial serif appears on a few pages (Endex, Limel). Light-weight, wide-tracked mono numerals.

## Signature moves

- Mono data rows laid out like spec sheets (label / value / unit)
- Dark hero with one soft blue radial glow behind a pricing or product block
- Isometric line illustrations on cream with a single orange accent
- One-accent phone UIs (lime on black, red on white)
- Hairline dividers and generous whitespace; dot-matrix LCD readouts
