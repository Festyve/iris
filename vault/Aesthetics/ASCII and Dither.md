# ASCII and Dither

Dithering, halftone, dot-matrix and ASCII-character imagery. Photos and objects (clouds, hands, bridges, fish, astronauts) are turned into pixel or dot grids, in 1-bit black and white or single-blue duotones. A smaller group are real web UIs: pixel koi chat interfaces, ASCII websites, a dither tool and dot-matrix headlines.

## Type

Monospace and dot-matrix/LED display faces, often huge (06% MATCH, BH OPS 2026), set against tiny mono captions. A few pieces use editorial condensed serif or grotesk headlines over dithered art.

## Signature moves

- 1-bit or duotone dithering of photos (Bayer or ordered dots) as hero art
- Dot-matrix numerals and type as the main graphic
- Pixelated sprites placed on clean light UI (koi in a chat app)
- ASCII-character fills that build shapes and textures
- Dither gradient bands (cream to navy) as backgrounds
- Mono terminal metadata rows framing the composition
