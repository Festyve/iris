# Bento Grids

Bento-box grids of rounded tiles in different sizes. Each tile holds one stat, image, CTA or feature, and they are assembled into landing pages, portfolios and posters. Many use high-contrast neon (lime and black, purple) or pastel tiles with large numbers and photography.

## Type

Grotesk sans (medium/semibold) with big stat numerals (78K, 57K, $230,545). Some display faces (wide neon "BENTO GRID", serif "Revolutionize"). Small label text inside tiles.

## Signature moves

- Asymmetric tile grid with consistent gutters and radius
- One idea per tile: stat, portrait, logo, CTA arrow
- Mixed fills: some tiles solid accent, some photo, some white
- Huge numerals as tile content with a small delta
- Rotated or stacked tile posters for portfolio showcase
