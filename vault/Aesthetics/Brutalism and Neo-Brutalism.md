# Brutalism and Neo-Brutalism

Two strands. Raw brutalism uses black-and-white HTML-like archives, link lists, Times New Roman and heavy black rules. Neo-brutalism uses bright flat fills (yellow, lilac, pink, lime), thick black outlines, hard offset shadows and cartoon mascots. Most items are real websites, mobile screens and component libraries.

## Type

Heavy condensed grotesk or wide display caps (WE DESIGN WITHOUT RULES, NEO BRUTALISM) next to default system serif (Times) and underlined blue links in the raw archives. Big weight contrast.

## Signature moves

- 2-3px black outlines on every card and button, with a hard offset drop shadow
- Flat saturated fills with no gradients; sticker-like cartoon mascots
- Raw HTML aesthetic: default links, serif text, hairline tables
- Heavy black bars and numbered blocks (01/02/03) in condensed type
- Dense multi-column layouts with visible borders
