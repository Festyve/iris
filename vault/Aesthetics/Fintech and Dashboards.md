# Fintech and Dashboards

Fintech, analytics and SaaS dashboards plus banking phone apps. Dark themes use lime, green or purple accents with dense charts. Light themes use pastel cards, big percentage numerals and rounded panels. Layouts are a sidebar, a KPI row and a chart grid, with phones shown as tilted or paired mockups.

## Type

Geometric/grotesk sans (Inter/Manrope-like). Very large light or medium numerals for KPIs ($7,854, 89%) over tiny grey labels. A few use mono for telemetry (Basalt, Horizontal View).

## Signature moves

- KPI hero numbers at 3-5× body size with small delta chips
- Rounded cards in a modular grid; one accent colour carries the data
- Bar and area charts with a highlighted active bar and tooltip
- Dark mode with lime/neon highlights; light mode with pastel tinted cards
- Phone banking cards with large balance and quick actions
