# Aurora, Gradients and Glow

Soft blurred gradients and glow: aurora ribbons, grainy mesh gradients, and dark backgrounds with a coloured bloom behind type or devices. Used as hero backgrounds for AI/tech landings, app screens and posters. A few off-theme items (isometric illustration, insect render, a koi pond photo) are included.

## Type

Mostly clean grotesk sans, light or regular, often white on dark. Some wide display caps (AURORAE, ARGUS) and occasional serif. Small mono captions on posters.

## Signature moves

- Large blurred colour bloom (radial or ribbon) behind a centred headline
- Film grain or noise layered on gradients
- Dark UI with glowing gradient fills inside cards or phone screens
- Wide-tracked display wordmark over a gradient field
- Gradient as a footer or section band fading into white
