# Motion and Interaction

These are still frames from motion and interaction websites: scroll carousels, 3D image rings, floating image fields, hover list reveals, layered perspective stacks and blurred type transitions. There are also several long illustrated or portrait portfolio templates. Motion is implied by blur, rotation, scattered or stacked layout and cursor states.

## Type

Grotesk sans at large sizes (OUR WORK, NOOMO LABS), editorial serifs in portfolio templates, and light list typography for hover menus.

## Signature moves

- Circular 3D carousels and rings of image cards
- Hover-reveal list where one item is bold and others fade
- Scattered floating images with depth
- Blurred or morphing type and image transitions
- Sticker labels on large headings
